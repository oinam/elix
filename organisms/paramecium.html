Fast facts

  • A Paramecium is one single cell, but it can swim, eat, and get rid of waste.
  • It is about 0.25 mm long. You need a microscope to see it.
  • It lives in ponds and puddles.
  • Its shape looks like the sole of a slipper.
  • Real ones have thousands of cilia!
