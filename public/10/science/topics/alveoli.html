🫧 Tiny air sacs

At the end of the smallest tubes: bunches of tiny air sacs, like grapes.

Fast facts

  • The tiny air sacs are called alveoli. Just one is an alveolus.
  • Your two lungs hold about 480 million of them!
  • Their walls, and the walls of the capillaries around them, are just one cell thick. Gases slip right through.
  • Red blood cells carry the oxygen away, to every cell in your body.
  • Carbon dioxide takes the same path, backwards: out of the blood, into the air sac, and out with your next breath.
  • Real blood is always red. Here, blue just shows blood that needs oxygen.
