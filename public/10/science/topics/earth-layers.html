Fast facts

  • It’s about 6,371 km from the surface to the center. Driving there at 60 km an hour would take more than 4 days.
  • The crust is the thinnest layer: about 5 to 10 km under the oceans, and 30 to 50 km under land. If Earth were an apple, the crust would be thinner than the skin.
  • The mantle is the thickest layer. Its rock is solid, but so hot it flows very, very slowly.
  • The outer core is liquid iron and nickel. As it swirls, it makes Earth’s magnetism, which is why a compass works.
  • The inner core is about 5,200 °C, nearly as hot as the surface of the Sun. It stays solid because it’s squeezed so hard.
  • Plates move about as fast as your fingernails grow: a few centimeters a year.
