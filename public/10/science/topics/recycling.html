Fast facts

  • The three Rs, best first: reduce (use less), reuse (use again), and recycle (make into something new).
  • Recycling saves trees, metal, and oil, and uses less energy than making things from scratch.
  • Glass and metal cans can be melted and made new again and again.
  • Food scraps and leaves rot into compost, which makes soil rich for plants.
  • Every town has its own rules for what goes in which bin, so check yours!
