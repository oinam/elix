🌳 Tubes inside a tree

A tree looks still. But inside, water and food are always on the move!

Tinker

Try this!

  • Cut the trunk open and find the two kinds of tubes.
  • Make it rain. Follow the water up to the leaves.
  • Add sunshine too. Where does the food go?
  • Watch the leaves breathe out water. (Rain and sun, then wait.)
  • Stop the rain and wait. What happens to the leaves?
  • Tap all 7 parts.

Fast facts

  • Plants with these tubes are called vascular plants. Trees, grass, and roses are all vascular. Mosses aren’t.
  • Xylem carries water and minerals up, from the roots to the leaves.
  • Phloem carries food (sugar) from the leaves to every part that needs it: down to the roots, and up to fruits and buds.
  • Leaves let water out through tiny holes. That pulls more water up, like sipping through a straw.
  • The tallest trees lift water more than 100 meters up, with no pump at all.
