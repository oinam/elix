    Fast facts

    • A terrarium is a closed jar with soil, water, air, and plants. It can keep going for years, because nothing leaves.
    • Inside, water goes round and round: it evaporates, condenses into drops on the glass, and falls like rain.
    • Plants take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen. Animals do the opposite. They swap!
    • An event is something that happens, like a storm or a volcano. One event can change all four spheres.
    • Earth works like a giant terrarium: the same water and air go round and round.
