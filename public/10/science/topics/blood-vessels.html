Fast facts

  • Arteries carry blood away from the heart. Their walls are thick and stretchy.
  • Veins bring blood back to the heart. Their walls are thin, and they have valves.
  • Capillaries are the tiniest. All the delivering happens there.
  • Arteries branch smaller and smaller as they go. Veins join up bigger and bigger on the way back.
  • All the blood vessels in a grown-up, end to end, could wrap around the Earth about two times!
  • Blood is a delivery truck: oxygen and food in, carbon dioxide and waste out.
