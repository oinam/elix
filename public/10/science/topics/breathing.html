🫁 The path of air

Breathe in, breathe out. Where does the air go?

Tinker

Try this!

  • Follow one breath all the way to the lungs.
  • Watch the diaphragm move. Tap it!
  • Slide the body to Running. How fast do you breathe?
  • Say “aaah”. Where does your voice come from?
  • Breathe in dusty air. What catches the dust?
  • Make the air smoky, then put on a mask.
  • Tap all 8 parts.

Fast facts

  • Your cells need oxygen to get energy out of food. That’s why you breathe!
  • Breathing in brings oxygen. Breathing out gets rid of carbon dioxide, a waste gas.
  • Swapping the two gases is called gas exchange. It happens in your lungs.
  • The diaphragm is a dome of muscle under your lungs. It does most of the work.
  • Resting, a kid breathes about 20 times a minute. You don’t even have to think about it.
  • Dirty air hurts lungs. Fewer fires and fumes, more trees, and a good mask on smoky days all help.
