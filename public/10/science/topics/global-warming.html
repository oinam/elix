🌡️ Global warming

Sunlight warms the Earth. The air holds some of that heat in, like a blanket.

Fast facts

  • Greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide and methane hold heat in the air. A little keeps Earth warm enough to live on.
  • Burning coal, oil, and gas in cars, factories, and power plants adds lots more carbon dioxide. Rotting garbage and farm animals add methane.
  • Since about 1850, Earth has warmed by more than 1 °C. That sounds small, but it’s enough to melt ice and change the weather.
  • Warming melts glaciers and ice caps, and warm water takes up more room, so the sea rises.
  • Heat waves, droughts, and very heavy rain are becoming more common.
  • Wind, solar, and water power, and planting forests, help slow it down.
