Fast facts

  • Light belongs to a big family called electromagnetic waves. From longest to shortest: radio, microwave, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma.
  • The shorter the wavelength, the higher the frequency, and the more energy the wave carries.
  • These waves need nothing to travel through. They cross empty space, like sunlight does.
  • They all go at the speed of light: about 300,000 km every second. That’s about 7½ times around the Earth!
  • Our eyes see only a tiny slice of the family: visible light.
