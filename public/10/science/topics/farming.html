Fast facts

  • People started farming about 10,000 years ago. Since then, many forests, grasslands, and swamps have been turned into fields.
  • Topsoil is the dark, rich top layer where plants grow. It can take hundreds of years to make just a few centimeters.
  • Roots hold soil together like a net. Without them, rain washes soil away and wind blows it off. That’s erosion.
  • Soil washed into rivers makes them muddy and harms fish.
  • Farmers protect soil with cover crops, rows of trees that block the wind, and by not leaving fields bare.
