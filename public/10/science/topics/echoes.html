Fast facts

  • An echo is sound that bounces off something and comes back.
  • Bats and dolphins find food by listening to echoes. That’s called echolocation.
  • Ships use sonar: they time an echo from the sea floor. Half the time, times the speed of sound in water (about 1,500 m/s), gives the depth.
  • Ultrasound is sound too high for people to hear. Doctors use it to see inside the body, safely.
  • Sound also carries music and talking, the most useful sounds of all!
