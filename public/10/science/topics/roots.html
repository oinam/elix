Fast facts

  • Roots hold a plant in the ground and soak up water and minerals.
  • In a root, xylem is in the middle and phloem sits around it.
  • Root hairs are so tiny and so many that they give a root a huge drinking surface.
  • Carrots, radishes, and beets are taproots. We eat them!
  • Scientists once measured one rye grass plant. Its roots added up to more than 600 kilometers!
  • A root cap covers the tip, like a helmet, as the root pushes through the soil.
