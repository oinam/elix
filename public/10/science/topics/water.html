Fast facts

  • Oceans cover about 71% of Earth’s surface, and hold about 97% of all its water.
  • Only about 3 cups out of 100 are fresh water, and most of that is frozen in glaciers and ice caps.
  • About 30% of fresh water hides underground in rocks and sand, called aquifers. Wells reach down to it.
  • Lakes and rivers are only a tiny bit of all the fresh water, about 1 drop in every 100.
  • Water goes round and round: the water you drink today might once have been drunk by a dinosaur!
