Fast facts

  • Transparent things let almost all light through, like clear glass. You can see right through them.
  • Translucent things let some light through, but scatter it, like wax paper or frosted glass. Things look blurry.
  • Opaque things let no light through, like wood. They make shadows.
  • Reflection: light bounces off a mirror at the same angle it hits it.
  • Refraction: light bends when it goes from water to air. That’s why a straw in water looks broken.
  • Light travels in straight lines, until something bounces or bends it.
