Fast facts

  • Pollution is anything that makes the air, water, or land dirty or harmful.
  • Burning fuel in cars, factories, and power plants gives off smoke and fumes that pollute the air.
  • Too much fertilizer washing into a river feeds a thick layer of algae. When the algae die and rot, the water loses its oxygen, and fish can’t breathe.
  • Oil spilled at sea coats the feathers of birds and the fur of animals, so they can’t stay warm or float.
  • Some trash rots in weeks. Plastic can last hundreds of years. Recycling and composting keep it out of landfills.
