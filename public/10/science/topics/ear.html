👂 The ear

How does a wiggle in the air become a sound in your head?

Play a sound

Try this!

  • Play a sound and follow it all the way to the brain.
  • Play a low sound. Which hair cells wiggle?
  • Play a high sound. Is it the same hair cells?
  • Play a sound that’s too loud.
  • Put on ear muffs for a loud sound.
  • Tap all 8 parts.

Fast facts

  • Your outer ear catches sound and sends it down the ear canal.
  • The eardrum is a thin skin, stretched like a drum. Sound makes it wiggle.
  • The three tiniest bones in your body are in your ear: the hammer, anvil, and stirrup.
  • The cochlea is shaped like a snail shell. It’s full of liquid and tiny hair cells.
  • High sounds wiggle hair cells near the start of the snail. Low sounds wiggle the ones near the end.
  • Very loud sounds can hurt hair cells for good. Turn it down, and cover your ears near loud noises.
