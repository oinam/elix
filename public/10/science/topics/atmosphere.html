Fast facts

  • Air is about 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen. The last 1% is mostly argon, with a little carbon dioxide and water vapor.
  • Gravity holds the air to the Earth. The higher you go, the thinner the air gets.
  • All our weather happens in the bottom layer, the troposphere. It’s about 12 km thick.
  • The ozone layer in the stratosphere soaks up most of the Sun’s harmful UV light, which causes sunburn.
  • The air is a warm blanket. Without it, Earth would average about −18 °C instead of about 15 °C.
