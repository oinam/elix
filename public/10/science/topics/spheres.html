Fast facts

  • Earth has four big systems, called spheres: the geosphere (rock), hydrosphere (water), atmosphere (air), and biosphere (life).
  • The names are old Greek words: geo means earth, hydro means water, atmo means vapor, and bio means life.
  • Earth is the only planet we know with liquid water on top, air we can breathe, and life. That’s what makes it special.
  • Air is mostly nitrogen (about 78%) and oxygen (about 21%).
  • The spheres share all the time: rain falls on rocks, plants drink water, and leaves give oxygen to the air.
