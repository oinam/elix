Fast facts

  • A lens telescope (refracting) bends light with lenses. A mirror telescope (reflecting) bounces it with curved mirrors.
  • Galileo pointed a telescope at the sky in 1609. He saw mountains on the Moon and moons around Jupiter!
  • Isaac Newton built one of the first mirror telescopes in 1668.
  • How much bigger things look = the telescope’s focal length ÷ the eyepiece’s focal length.
  • Radio telescopes catch radio waves from space. India’s GMRT, near Pune, has 30 giant dishes.
  • The biggest space telescopes, like James Webb, use mirrors. Big mirrors gather lots of light.
