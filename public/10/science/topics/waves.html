🌊 What is a wave?

A wave carries energy from place to place. Let’s make some!

Pick a wave

Tinker

Try this!

  • Shake the rope. Watch the red bead. Does it travel along?
  • Push the slinky. See the coils bunch up and spread out.
  • Tap the pond to drop a pebble.
  • Make a ripple reach the duck. Does it float away?
  • Shake hard. What changes?
  • Tap all 7 parts.

Fast facts

  • A wave is a wiggle that repeats. It carries energy from one place to another.
  • The stuff itself (rope, water, air) only wiggles in place. It doesn’t travel with the wave.
  • In a transverse wave, things wiggle up and down, across the way the wave goes. Light works like this.
  • In a longitudinal wave, things wiggle back and forth, the same way the wave goes. Sound works like this.
  • A bigger shake puts in more energy, so you get a bigger wave.
