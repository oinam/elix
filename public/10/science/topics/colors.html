Fast facts

  • White light is all the colors mixed: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet.
  • Red has the longest wavelength we can see (about 700 nanometers). Violet has the shortest (about 400).
  • A prism bends each color a different amount, so the colors fan out. Isaac Newton did this in 1666!
  • Things look colored because they reflect some colors and absorb the rest. A red apple reflects red.
  • White things reflect all colors. Black things absorb them all.
  • Mixing light isn’t like mixing paint: red and green light make yellow!
