Fast facts

  • Every sound starts with something vibrating: a string, a drum, or the vocal cords in your throat.
  • Sound needs something to travel through, like air, water, or a solid. In empty space, there’s no sound.
  • Pitch is how high or low a sound is. More vibrations each second (higher frequency) means a higher pitch.
  • Loudness comes from amplitude. Loudness is measured in decibels (dB).
  • In air, sound goes about 343 meters each second. In water, about 1,480. In steel, about 5,900!
  • Light is much faster than sound. That’s why you see lightning before you hear the thunder.
