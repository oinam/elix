Fast facts

  • The tubes from the root keep going, all the way up the stem.
  • Cambium is a thin layer that makes new cells: xylem on its inside, phloem on its outside.
  • In a woody stem, the tubes make rings. In a soft stem, they come in little bundles.
  • A tree grows one light ring (spring) and one dark ring (late summer) every year. Count them to tell its age!
  • A thin ring means a dry year, with little rain.
  • Some bristlecone pine trees are more than 4,800 years old.
