📏 Parts of a wave

Every wave has the same parts. Change the wave and watch them!

Tinker

Try this!

  • Make the amplitude as big as it goes.
  • Make the sea calm.
  • Turn up the frequency. What happens to the wavelength?
  • Pause the wave and tap the wavelength.
  • Build a sandcastle, then wash it away with a big wave.
  • Tap all 7 parts.

Fast facts

  • The top of a wave is the crest. The bottom is the trough.
  • Wavelength is the distance from one crest to the next.
  • Amplitude is how far the wave goes up from the calm water line. Bigger amplitude, more energy!
  • Frequency is how many waves go past each second. It’s measured in hertz (Hz).
  • When waves travel at the same speed, more waves each second means each one is shorter.
  • For sound, amplitude is loudness. For light, it’s brightness.
