Fast facts

  • Your body is made of trillions of cells, and each one has a job.
  • Cells follow rules: grow, rest, and stop when there’s no more room.
  • Cancer is when some cells stop following the rules and keep on growing.
  • Cancer is not catching. You can’t get it from hugging, sharing, or playing with someone.
  • Every day, your body fixes or clears away most damaged cells all by itself.
  • Doctors have many ways to help, like medicines and operations, and many people get better.
  • Sometimes a family passes down an instruction book that breaks a bit more easily. That’s nobody’s fault.
  • Look after your cells: hats, shade, and sunscreen in strong sun, good food, lots of play, and never smoke.
