🍃 Leaves make food

A leaf is a food factory. Let’s look inside!

  • 💧 Water
  • 💨 Carbon dioxide

Makes: 🍬 sugar and 🫧 oxygen

Fast facts

  • Making food from sunlight is called photosynthesis: “making with light.”
  • Chlorophyll is the green stuff in chloroplasts. It traps sunlight. That’s why leaves are green!
  • The tiny holes are stomata. Just one is a stoma. Most are on the underside of the leaf.
  • One leaf can have thousands of stomata.
  • Plants (and tiny ocean algae) make the oxygen you breathe.
  • Plants store extra sugar as starch. A potato is full of it!
