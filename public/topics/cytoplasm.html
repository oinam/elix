Fast facts

  • Cytoplasm is the jelly that fills a cell, between the membrane and the nucleus. It’s mostly water.
  • All the cell’s little parts float in it. They’re called organelles, which means “little organs”.
  • Lots of the cell’s work happens in the jelly. The first step of breaking down sugar happens right there.
  • In plant cells, the cytoplasm flows round and round. You can see it move in a Hydrilla leaf under a microscope!
