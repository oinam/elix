Fast facts

  • Most living things you can see are made of many cells. You have trillions!
  • Cells of the same kind team up into a tissue.
  • Different tissues team up into an organ, like the heart, the brain, or a leaf.
  • Organs team up into an organ system, like the circulatory system or the shoot system.
  • All the systems together make an organism: one whole living thing.
