❤️ The heart

Your heart is a pump with four rooms. It never takes a day off!

Tinker

Try this!

  • Follow one blood cell all the way around.
  • Listen to the heart. What sound does it make?
  • Slow the heart down, like when you’re sleeping.
  • Make it race, like after a sprint.
  • Tap the left ventricle. Why is its wall so thick?
  • Tap all 9 parts.

Fast facts

  • Your heart is about the size of your fist.
  • It has four rooms: two atria on top, and two ventricles below.
  • The right side sends blood to the lungs. The left side sends it to the whole body.
  • The “lub-dub” sound is the valves snapping shut, like doors.
  • A kid’s heart beats about 70 to 100 times a minute when resting.
  • On this model, the heart’s right side is on your left, as if it’s facing you.
