Fast facts

  • Four jobs: ingestion (eating), digestion (breaking food down), absorption (soaking it into the blood), and elimination (getting rid of waste).
  • In a grown-up, the whole tube is about 9 meters long, all coiled up inside!
  • Your stomach makes acid strong enough to break down food. A slimy coat of mucus keeps it from hurting the stomach.
  • Your mouth makes up to about a liter of saliva a day.
  • Food takes about one to three days to go all the way through.
  • Fruits, veggies, and whole grains have fiber. It keeps food moving along.
