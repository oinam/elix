Fast facts

  • Your body is made of trillions of cells.
  • The cell membrane lets some things in and keeps others out. Scientists say it’s selectively permeable.
  • The nucleus is the control center. It has its own membrane, with tiny holes called pores.
  • Chromosomes in the nucleus carry genes, which pass from parents to children. That’s one reason you may look like your parents!
  • Plant cells also have a hard cell wall outside the membrane. Animal cells don’t.
