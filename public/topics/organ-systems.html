Fast facts

  • Tissues team up into an organ, like the heart, a lung, or a leaf.
  • Organs team up into an organ system. The heart and blood vessels make the circulatory system.
  • Your heart beats about 100,000 times every day.
  • A plant has two systems: the root system under the ground and the shoot system above it.
  • Xylem carries water up. Phloem carries food to every part that needs it.
  • No system works alone. Food gives you energy only because your blood carries it, and your lungs bring the oxygen to use it.
