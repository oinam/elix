    Light’s limit

    Fast facts

    • Your eyes can’t see anything much thinner than a hair. Most cells are far smaller.
    • A microscope uses lenses to make tiny things look bigger.
    • To find how much bigger, multiply the two lenses: 10 × 40 = 400 times.
    • Always start with the smallest lens, find the picture, then switch up.
    • The picture in a microscope is upside down and back to front!
    • Light can’t show anything much smaller than the smallest bacteria. To see viruses and atoms, we need electron microscopes.
