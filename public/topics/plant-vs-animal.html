Fast facts

  • Plant cells have a hard cell wall outside the membrane. It keeps them firm and boxy.
  • Only plant cells have chloroplasts, the green parts that make food from sunlight.
  • A plant cell has one big vacuole. Animal cells have small ones.
  • Animal cells have centrioles. Most plant cells don’t.
  • Both have a nucleus, a cell membrane, cytoplasm, and mitochondria.
