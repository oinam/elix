Fast facts

  • A single-cell organism is a whole living thing made of just one cell.
  • That one cell does every job: moving, eating, getting rid of waste, and making more.
  • Most are too small to see without a microscope.
  • They live almost everywhere: ponds, soil, food, and even inside you!
