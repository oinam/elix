Fast facts

  • A system is a group of parts that work together to do a job.
  • Take away one part, and the whole system can stop working.
  • A part’s shape (structure) fits its job (function). Round wheels roll. A chain bends around the gears.
  • A bike frame is made of triangles, one of the strongest shapes.
  • Systems are everywhere: a clock, a tree, a school, and your own body!
  • Your body has smaller systems inside it, like your heart and blood vessels. See them team up in Organs and organ systems.
