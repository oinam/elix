Fast facts

  • Your body has more than 200 kinds of cells. Each kind has a shape that fits its job.
  • Almost every cell has a membrane, cytoplasm, and a nucleus. Grown-up red blood cells give up their nucleus, to make room for more oxygen.
  • Blood is never blue! It’s dark red without oxygen, and bright red with it.
  • A red blood cell lives for about 4 months, and goes around your whole body many times a day.
  • Some nerve cells reach from your back all the way to your toes: about 1 meter long!
  • A muscle cell is so long that it has many nuclei, not just one.
  • White blood cells crawl and swallow germs, a lot like an Amoeba.
