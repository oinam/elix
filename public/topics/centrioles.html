Fast facts

  • Centrioles come in pairs, set at right angles to each other, next to the nucleus.
  • Each one is a ring of 9 bundles, with 3 tiny tubes in each bundle.
  • Before a cell splits, the pair makes a copy, so each new cell gets one pair.
  • Animal cells have centrioles. Most plant cells don’t, but they still split!
  • The same kind of tube sits at the root of cilia, like the tiny hairs on a Paramecium.
