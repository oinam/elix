Fast facts

  • Cells get energy from food and oxygen: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy. That’s cellular respiration.
  • The digestive system brings the glucose. The respiratory system brings the oxygen.
  • The circulatory system carries both to every cell, and takes the carbon dioxide back to the lungs.
  • Like runners in a relay race, each system passes the baton to the next.
  • Almost nothing in your body is done by one system alone.
