Fast facts

  • Bacteria are single cells, and they are the tiniest living things. Thousands could fit on the dot of this i.
  • They have no nucleus. Their DNA floats loose in the cell.
  • They make more bacteria by splitting in two. In warm food, some can double every 20 minutes!
  • Helpful bacteria turn milk into curd and live in your tummy to help digest food.
  • Harmful ones can make you sick. Washing hands, boiling water, and keeping food cold keep them away.
