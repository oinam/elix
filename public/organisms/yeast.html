Fast facts

  • Yeast is a fungus, a cousin of mushrooms. But each yeast is just one cell.
  • It makes more yeast by budding: a baby cell grows out of the parent and breaks off.
  • It eats sugar and gives off carbon dioxide gas. Changing sugar like this is called fermentation.
  • The gas bubbles make bread dough rise. That's why bread is soft and full of tiny holes!
  • Dry yeast from the shop is asleep. Warm water and sugar wake it up. The oven's heat kills it.
