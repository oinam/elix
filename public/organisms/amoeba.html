Fast facts

  • An Amoeba is one single cell. It lives in ponds, often on the mud at the bottom.
  • It has no fixed shape. It keeps changing shape as it moves.
  • It moves and catches food with false feet (pseudopodia).
  • It makes more Amoebas by splitting in two.
  • A few kinds of Amoeba can make people sick through dirty water. That’s one reason to drink clean water!
